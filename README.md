# 🛒 Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard

**Sales • Profit • Products • Discounts • Geographic Performance**

An interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze Supermart grocery sales data and help management understand overall business performance, identify profitable products, evaluate discount strategies, and compare geographic sales performance.

**Project:** Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard  
**Organization:** SapphireIQ – Brilliant Minds. Intelligent Solutions.  
**Project Type:** Data Analytics and Business Intelligence

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Supermart Grocery Sales Dashboard transforms raw grocery sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and performance indicators.

The dashboard enables management to monitor sales and profit, identify high-performing product categories, analyze sales trends over time, understand the relationship between discounts and profitability, and evaluate the performance of different states, cities, and regions.

Instead of manually reviewing the complete dataset, users can explore key business metrics in one place and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

### Objective 1: Understand Overall Sales Performance

**Business Question:** How is the overall grocery business performing?

- Calculate total sales and total profit.
- Identify product categories contributing the largest share of total sales.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Identify regions generating the highest sales.

### Objective 2: Understand Product Performance

**Business Question:** Which products and categories are driving sales and profit?

- Compare sales across product categories and sub-categories.
- Identify products generating the highest sales.
- Analyze products with high sales but comparatively low profit.
- Evaluate product-level performance to identify improvement opportunities.

### Objective 3: Understand Profit and Discount

**Business Question:** How are discounts and profit related to business performance?

- Identify categories and regions generating the highest profit.
- Compare sales and profitability across business segments.
- Analyze the relationship between discount levels and profit.
- Identify products or categories where high discounts may be associated with lower profitability.

### Objective 4: Understand Geographic Performance

**Business Question:** Which locations are contributing most to the business?

- Identify states and cities generating the highest sales.
- Compare sales and profit across geographic locations.
- Identify locations with high sales but comparatively low profit.
- Highlight geographic areas that may require further business analysis.
